Materials database

Every part, with its size, colour and safety

Companies and businesses contribute salvaged parts. Each one is recorded with its type, size, colour, condition and safety check. Generators draw only on stock that is available and cleared.

Demo data. The records are synthetic, generated from the same part catalogue by scripts/generate-parts.mjs. Contributor names are placeholders.

Data model 
part_types       type, name, plural, material, planning_kg, co2_per_kg
contributors     id, name, kind, zone
parts            id, type, contributor_id, zone, condition, safety, status, kg, added_on
part_dimensions  part_id, length_mm, width_mm, height_mm
part_colours     part_id, hex, name, share
part_tags        part_id, tag
part_assets      part_id, kind (photo | mesh | thumb), uri
safety_checks    part_id, check_name, result, checked_by, checked_at
available_stock  view: cleared and available parts per type, with kg and CO₂e
part_impact      view: CO₂e avoidable per part

db/schema.sql defines these tables. db/seed.sql loads this dataset.
